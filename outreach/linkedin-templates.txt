2. "licensed contractor" "kitchen remodel" "Denver"
3. "construction company owner" "Phoenix"
4. "project manager" "residential construction" "Seattle"
5. "roofing contractor" "Dallas"
6. "deck builder" "Portland"
7. "HVAC contractor" "Atlanta"
8. "licensed plumber" "Miami"
9. "electrician" "Nashville"
10. "home remodeler" "San Diego"

### Advanced Filters
- **Industry**: Construction
- **Company headcount**: 1-50
- **Title**: Owner, Founder, Project Manager, Superintendent, Operations Manager
- **Location**: Target cities with active construction
- **Groups**: Member of local contractor associations (e.g., NAHB, local home builder associations)

## Daily Outreach Routine

### Morning (30 min)
- 20 LinkedIn connection requests
- 10 personalized InMails
- Review and respond to any replies

### Afternoon (30 min)
- 20 cold emails (use Email 1 template)
- 10 Twitter/Facebook DMs to contractors
- Follow up on any pending conversations

### Weekly (2 hours)
- Engage with 20 contractor posts (like, comment thoughtfully)
- Share 2-3 posts on your own feed
- Update CRM (Google Sheet) with leads and status

### Expected Results
- 100 touches/day × 22 days = 2,200/month
- 1-2% response rate = 22-44 responses
- 20-30% of responses book demo = 4-13 demos
- 30-40% of demos convert = 1-5 customers/week
- **Month 1 target**: 10-20 customers
