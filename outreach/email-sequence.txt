## Targeting Criteria

### Ideal Contractor Profile
- **Company size**: 2-20 employees
- **Specialty**: Residential remodelers, general contractors, deck builders, kitchen & bath, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical
- **Location**: Suburban areas with active construction (search "permit delays in [City]" on LinkedIn/Twitter)
- **Signals**: 
  - Posts about projects or jobs
  - Mentions permit frustrations
  - Active in local contractor groups

### Where to Find Them
1. **LinkedIn**: Search "general contractor [City]" or "licensed contractor [City]"
2. **Nextdoor / Facebook Groups**: Local contractor groups
3. **Houzz Pro / BuildZoom**: Contractor directories
4. **Twitter/X**: Search "permit" + "[City]" to find complaining contractors
5. **Angi / HomeAdvisor**: Top-rated contractors in your area

### Outreach Volume
- **Goal**: 100 prospects/day
- **Breakdown**:
  - 50 LinkedIn connection requests
  - 30 cold emails
  - 20 Twitter/Facebook DMs
- **Expected conversion**: 1-2% to demo → 1-2 customers per day → 30-60 customers in first month

## Objection Handling

### "We already have a permit person / process"
> That's great. PermitAI isn't designed to replace your permit specialist — it's designed to make them 10x more productive. Most of our customers use it to handle 80% of routine permits so their team can focus on complex projects.

### "We don't do enough projects to justify the cost"
> That's exactly why we built PermitAI. The Solo plan is $49/month — less than 1 hour of labor. On your next project, it'll save you 10+ hours and likely prevent a rejected submission that costs $500+ in fees and delays.

### "I don't trust AI for permit applications"
> Understandable. That's why we built a "Review Before Submit" workflow. AI drafts the application, but you (or your permit specialist) review and approve before submission. Our 92% first-pass rate speaks for itself.

### "We're too busy to try something new"
> I hear that a lot. That's exactly why PermitAI exists — because you're too busy to spend 15 hours on permits. The setup takes 5 minutes, and the AI does the rest. You can try it free for 14 days with zero commitment.
