## Tips for Generating Mockups

1. **Use DALL-E 3 or Midjourney v6** for best text rendering
2. **Generate multiple variations** of each prompt and pick the best
3. **Iterate**: Use the "Vary" feature to refine designs
4. **Add real screenshots**: Once you build the MVP, take real screenshots of the UI and use those for marketing
5. **Tools for post-processing**: Canva (free), Figma (free), or Photoshop
